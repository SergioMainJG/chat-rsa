What our server sees

What it cannot see

  • The content of your messages: it stores only the ciphertext.
  • Your private keys: they never leave your device.
  • What you search for: search runs on your device.

What it does see

  • Your user name and who is in each room.
  • The names of the rooms.
  • When each message is sent and how far each member has read.
  • Your public keys.
  • Your IP address while you use the service, to limit abuse.
